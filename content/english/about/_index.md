## Hey, I'm Hugo

I'm a backend engineer based in **Malmö, Sweden**. I specialize in building high-throughput systems, designing scalable architectures, and helping teams adopt modern infrastructure practices.

I studied nano- and high-speed electronics at **Lund University**, which gave me a deep appreciation for performance, efficiency, and understanding systems at a fundamental level. That perspective shapes how I approach software—always thinking about what's happening under the hood.

## What I Enjoy

Like cargo ships, I enjoy **containers**—especially when they're orchestrated by Kubernetes. I build backend services that handle real-world scale, working primarily in **Go**, **Python**, and **Node.js**.

I'm most engaged when working on:

- **High-throughput APIs** that need to handle millions of requests
- **Distributed systems** with complex data flows
- **Database optimization** for large-scale data
- **Cloud infrastructure** that's reliable and cost-effective

## Current Work

I'm currently a **Backend Engineer at Amberdata**, building high-performance services for digital asset data infrastructure.

I also take on freelance consulting projects, helping companies with backend architecture, system design, and infrastructure modernization.
